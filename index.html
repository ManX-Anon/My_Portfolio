About

I'm a B.Tech Cybersecurity student focused on offensive security fundamentals — enumeration, exploitation, and access-control abuse — alongside blue-team basics like log analysis and detection. Most of my practical work centers on broken access control: IDOR, horizontal and vertical privilege escalation, and unprotected admin functionality, along with Linux privilege-escalation chains involving SUID misuse, cron misconfiguration, and group-membership pivots.

I've also completed cybersecurity job simulations with Telstra, Tata, and Mastercard, and worked through SOC fundamentals on LetsDefend. Outside of labs, I'm building a Linux shell from scratch in C++ to understand process control, signals, and job management at the OS level.

I'm working toward roles in security engineering, penetration testing, and ethical hacking, with a longer-term interest in network security, threat analysis, and digital forensics.
